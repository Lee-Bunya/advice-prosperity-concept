Falcon Financial Advisers

Live in four months.The whole team in by day three.

Falcon mapped how it really gives advice, built the system round it and moved every client across.

4 monthsFrom the day Falcon signed on, in late June 2026, to going live on Monday 19 October.
Day 3The whole team was working in it: partners, advisers, paraplanner and client services.
75 stepsFalcon’s own advice process, mapped and built in. They call it The Falcon Engine.

Before

Falcon’s advice work ran across five places, and the team stitched them together by hand. Work was tracked through a weekly WIP list, daily huddles and to-do lists sent by email.

  • SharePoint
  • Xplan
  • Confluence
  • A spreadsheet
  • Email

What changed

Falcon and Advice Prosperity mapped the practice’s advice process into 75 steps, then set Advice Prosperity up to match. Every client, SOA, ROA, review and task now sits at one of those steps.

By day three after go-live, the whole team was using it and every existing client had been placed at their current step.
